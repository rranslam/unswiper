# Privacy

- Unswiper works **entirely on your computer**. It has no account, no cloud, and sends no photos or data anywhere.
- It only reads the folder you choose.
- Its own records (your decisions and commit history) are stored in `~/.local/share/unswiper` (or `$XDG_DATA_HOME/unswiper`). Thumbnails are cached in `~/.cache/unswiper`.
- Files are only moved when you confirm a commit, and only to the Trash or a quarantine folder — Unswiper never permanently deletes anything.
- **Photo Info** shows the location saved in a photo only on this computer. It is never sent anywhere or saved in Unswiper's records; the map button opens OpenStreetMap in your browser only when you click it. You can hide locations with **Show Location in Photo Info** in the main menu.
