# Getting Started

Unswiper helps you **pare down your photos and videos one visit at a time**. Instead of swiping through thousands of pictures, you open one trip or outing, keep the best few, and move the rest out of the way.

## Step 1 — Choose your photo folder

1. Open Unswiper (from the app grid / Activities, or your desktop's application menu).
2. Click **Choose Library Folder…**.
3. In the folder picker, select the folder that holds your photos (for example **Pictures**, or a folder you copied from your phone or camera), then click **Select**.

Tip: the first time, try a **copy** of a small folder so you can get comfortable before using your real library.

## Step 2 — Let it index

Unswiper now **indexes** the folder. That means it looks at every photo and video, reads the date it was taken, and groups them into visits. A spinner and a count such as `120 media / 300 files` show progress. Large folders can take a few minutes.

Your photos are **not moved or changed** during indexing. While Unswiper is open it also **watches the folder**: photos you add, rename or delete show up in the visits list by themselves.

## Step 3 — Pick a visit and reduce it

When indexing finishes you'll see a list of **visits**. Click one to open it, mark the photos you want to **Keep** and the ones to **Reject**, then click **Review & Commit…** to move that visit's rejects. See the other help topics for each step.

## Changing folders later

- Open the main menu (the **☰** button at the top right) and choose **Choose Folder…**.
- To rescan the same folder, choose **Re-index** or press **Ctrl+Shift+R**.
