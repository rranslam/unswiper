# Photo Info

Photo Info shows how a photo was taken: the camera, the settings, the date, the size and, if the photo has one, the place. It helps you choose between similar shots. Looking at the info **never changes your files**.

## Quick look: right-click

1. Open a visit (click it in the list on the left).
2. **Right-click** a photo or video.

A small box opens next to it with the main facts: camera and lens, shutter speed, aperture, ISO, focal length, date, size, file type and how sharp Unswiper thinks the photo is. Click anywhere else (or press **Esc**) to close it. Click **Show All Info** to open the full panel for that item.

## The Photo Info panel

- Press **I** (or **Ctrl+I**) to show or hide the panel on the right. You can also open the main menu **☰** and choose **Show Photo Info**.
- The panel shows the item you **selected**. If several are selected, it shows the first one and says how many more are selected.
- It also works in the viewer: it follows along when you press **←** or **→**.
- The panel stays open or closed until you quit Unswiper.

## The line under the grid

When an item is selected, a short line under the grid sums up its settings, for example **26 mm · f/1.8 · 1/120 s · ISO 64 · Sharp**. Click another photo to compare. The line always shows the **selected** item only.

## What the rows mean

- **Camera** and **Lens** — the camera (or phone) and the lens it used.
- **Shutter** — how long the picture was exposed, for example **1/120 s**. Long times such as **1/8 s** blur easily if the camera or subject moved.
- **Aperture** — the lens opening, for example **f/1.8**. A small number means a blurrier background.
- **ISO** — the sensor sensitivity. High numbers (above about 1600) mean a darker scene and more grain.
- **Focal length** — the zoom. For phones the "equiv." value is what it would be on a classic 35 mm camera.
- **Flash** and **Exposure compensation** — whether the flash fired, and whether the photo was set brighter (+) or darker (−).
- A **—** means the camera didn't record that value. Screenshots and edited copies often have no camera rows at all.
- **Taken** — the date and time the photo was taken, with the time zone when the camera saved it. Otherwise it says **time zone unknown**.
- **Dimensions** — megapixels and width × height. **Size** includes the motion part of a Live Photo, for example **2.1 MB + IMG_1234.MOV, 3.4 MB**.
- **Sharpness** — **Sharp**, **Soft** or **Blurry**, from Unswiper's blur check (see *Finding Blurry Photos*). **Not checked** means the check hasn't run yet, is turned off, or doesn't apply (videos, screenshots, very dark photos).

## Location

Many phones save **where** a photo was taken. If they did, the panel shows the **Coordinates** (and the altitude).

- The location is read from the photo and shown **only on this computer**. Unswiper never sends it anywhere and doesn't save it in its own records.
- The map button next to the coordinates opens the place on **OpenStreetMap** in your web browser. That is the only time the location leaves your computer, and only when you click it.
- To hide locations, open the main menu **☰** and turn off **Show Location in Photo Info**. Unswiper remembers this.
- Unswiper never changes or removes the location stored in your photos.
