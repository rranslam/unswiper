# Live Photos and Sidecar Files

An iPhone **Live Photo** is really two files: a still picture (for example `IMG_1234.HEIC`) and a short video with the same name (`IMG_1234.MOV`). Editing apps may also add small helper files with the same name, such as `.AAE` or `.XMP` ("sidecar" files).

Unswiper treats these as **one item**. You only see the photo, and when you keep or reject it, its matching video and sidecar files are kept or moved along with it. Restore brings them all back together.
