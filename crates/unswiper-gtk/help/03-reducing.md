# Reducing a Visit

Click a visit in the list to open it. You'll see a grid of its photos and videos.

## Keep or Reject

1. Click a photo to select it. Hold **Ctrl** and click to add more photos to the selection, or **Shift**-click to select a range.
2. Click **Keep** (or press **K**) for photos you want to keep.
3. Click **Reject** (or press **R**) for photos you want to remove.
4. Click **Clear** (or press **U**) to undo the choice.

Nothing is moved yet — these are only decisions. They are saved automatically.

## Keep target

At the top you'll see **Keep** with a number. This is how many photos you'd like to end up with from this visit. Each visit has its own target. When you reach it you'll see **Keep target reached**. It's only a guide — it never deletes anything by itself.

## Filter

Use the **All / Photos / Videos** buttons at the top to show everything, only photos, or only videos.
