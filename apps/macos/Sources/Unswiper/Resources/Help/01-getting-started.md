# Getting Started

Unswiper helps you **pare down your photos and videos one visit at a time**. Instead of swiping through thousands of pictures, you open one trip or outing, keep the best few, and move the rest out of the way.

## Step 1 — Choose your photo folder

1. Open Unswiper.
2. Click **Choose Library Folder…**.
3. In the window that appears, click the folder that holds your photos (for example **Pictures**, or a folder you copied from your phone or camera), then click **Open**.

Tip: the first time, try a **copy** of a small folder so you can get comfortable before using your real library.

Your photos are in **Google Photos**? Click **Import from Google Photos…** instead (on the start screen, or **Library ▸ Import from Google Photos…** in the menu bar). It guides you through downloading them with Google Takeout; see the help topic **Import from Google Photos**.

## Step 2 — Let it index

Unswiper now **indexes** the folder. That means it looks at every photo and video, reads the date it was taken, and groups them into visits. A small spinner and a count such as `120 media / 300 files` show progress. Large folders can take a few minutes.

Your photos are **not moved or changed** during indexing.

## Step 3 — Pick a visit and reduce it

When indexing finishes, the list of **visits** appears on the left side of the window. Click one and its photos open on the right, next to the list. Mark the photos you want to **Keep** and the ones to **Reject**, then click **Review & commit…**. To get more room, hide the list with the sidebar button at the top left or **View ▸ Hide Sidebar** (**⌃⌘S**). See the other help topics for each step.

## Changing folders later

- Click the **⋯** button at the top of the visits list and choose **Change Folder…**, or choose **Library ▸ Choose Folder…** from the menu bar at the top of the screen.
- To rescan the same folder, choose **Re-index** in the **⋯** menu or press **⇧⌘R** (Shift + Command + R).
