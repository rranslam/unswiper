# Keyboard Shortcuts

On a Mac, **⌘** is the Command key, **⇧** is the Shift key and **⌥** is the Option key.

The single keys work whenever a visit is open, also right after you clicked in the visits list, but not while you type in a text box. They're also listed in the **Photo** menu.

- **K** — Keep the selected photos (in a visit)
- **R** — Reject the selected photos (in a visit)
- **U** — Clear the choice for the selected photos (also clears a **Blurry?** suggestion for good)
- **Space** or **double-click** — Open the selected photo or video in the viewer (Space or **Esc** closes it)
- **← / →** — In the photo grid, select the previous or next photo
- **↑ / ↓** — In the photo grid, select the photo one row up or down
- **Fn+↑ / Fn+↓ (Page Up / Page Down)** — In the photo grid, move the selection a screenful up or down
- **Fn+← / Fn+→ (Home / End)** — In the photo grid, select the first or last photo
- The grid scrolls to keep the selected photo in view. The selection doesn't wrap around at either end.
- **⇧, ⌘ or ⌥ with ↑ / ↓** — Unswiper doesn't use these; macOS scrolls the grid instead, without changing the selection (⌥↑ / ⌥↓ jump to the top or bottom)
- **← / →** — In the viewer, show the previous or next item
- **I** or **⌘I** — Show or hide the Photo Info inspector (camera, settings, date, size, location)
- **Right-click ▸ Show Info** — Quick look at a photo's Photo Info
- **⌘ Return** — Open Review & commit (it still asks you to confirm)
- **Esc** — Cancel the Review & commit screen
- **⇧⌘R** — Re-index the current folder
- **⌃⌘S** — Hide or show the visits list (⌃ is the Control key)
- **⌘?** — Open Unswiper Help
- **⌘W** — Close the current window
- **⌘Q** — Quit Unswiper
