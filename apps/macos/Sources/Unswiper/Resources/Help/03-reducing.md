# Reducing a Visit

Click a visit in the list to open it. You'll see a grid of its photos and videos.

## Keep or Reject

1. Click a photo to select it (a highlight appears). Click more photos to add them to the selection; click a selected photo again to unselect it.
2. Click **Keep** (or press **K**) for photos you want to keep.
3. Click **Reject** (or press **R**) for photos you want to remove.
   Click **Clear** (or press **U**) to undo the choice.
4. Right-click a photo for **Keep**, **Reject** or **Clear** (undo the choice).

Nothing is moved yet — these are only decisions. They are saved automatically.

## Keep target

At the top you'll see **Keep** with a number and up/down arrows. This is how many photos you'd like to end up with from this visit. Each visit has its own target. When you reach it you'll see **Keep target reached**. It's only a guide — it never deletes anything by itself.

## Compare

Select 2 to 4 similar photos and click **Compare** to see them side by side, larger. Click **Keep**, **Reject** or **Clear** under each one, then **Done**.

## Filter

Use the **All / Photos / Videos / Blurry** buttons at the top to show everything, only photos, only videos, or only the photos Unswiper suggested as blurry (see **Blurry Photos**).
