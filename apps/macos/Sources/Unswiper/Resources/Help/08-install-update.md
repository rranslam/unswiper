# Install, Update and Uninstall

## Install

1. Go to **https://github.com/rranslam/unswiper/releases/latest** in Safari.
2. Under **Assets**, click the file ending in **.dmg** (for example `Unswiper-0.4.0.dmg`). Don't click "Source code".
3. Open **Finder**, click **Downloads** in the sidebar, and double-click the `.dmg` file.
4. Drag the **Unswiper** icon onto the **Applications** folder icon.
5. In the Finder sidebar, click the eject button (⏏) next to **Unswiper**.

## Opening it the first time ("Open Anyway")

Unswiper isn't signed with a paid Apple developer account, so macOS blocks it the first time. This is expected.

**macOS 15 (Sequoia) or newer:**

1. Double-click Unswiper in **Applications**. When the warning appears, click **Done** (not "Move to Trash").
2. Click the **Apple menu ()** ▸ **System Settings…** ▸ **Privacy & Security**.
3. Scroll to the bottom. Next to *"Unswiper" was blocked*, click **Open Anyway**.
4. Enter your Mac password (or use Touch ID), then click **Open Anyway** again.

**macOS 14 (Sonoma):** Control-click (or right-click) Unswiper in Applications, choose **Open**, then click **Open**.

## Updating

1. Quit Unswiper (**⌘Q**).
2. Download and install the new `.dmg` the same way. When asked, click **Replace**.
3. You may need to do "Open Anyway" again.
4. After updating, click **Re-index** once.

Your decisions and settings are kept.

## Uninstalling

1. Quit Unswiper, then drag it from **Applications** to the Trash.
2. To remove its saved data too: in Finder choose **Go ▸ Go to Folder…**, type `~/Library/Application Support/` and press Return, then drag the **Unswiper** folder to the Trash.

Uninstalling never touches your photos.
