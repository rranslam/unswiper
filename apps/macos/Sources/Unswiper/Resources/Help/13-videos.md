# Watching Videos and Large Previews

You can play a video, or look at a photo in a large view, without leaving Unswiper. Playing or looking **never changes your files**.

## Opening the viewer

1. Open a visit (click it on the Visits screen).
2. Video tiles show a **▶** badge with the length of the clip, for example **▶ 0:07**.
3. Do one of these:
   - **Double-click** a photo or video, or
   - click it once to select it, then press the **Space** bar.

The viewer fills the window. The bar at the top shows the file name and where you are, for example **3 of 40**.

## Inside the viewer

- **Space** or **Esc** — close the viewer and go back to the grid (or click **Grid** at the top left).
- **← / →** (Left and Right arrow keys) — the previous or next item in the visit. The round arrow buttons at the sides do the same.
- **K** Keep, **R** Reject, **U** Clear — work on the item you are looking at, just like in the grid. Its badge at the top changes so you can see your choice.
- Videos have the usual controls at the bottom: play/pause, a bar to jump to any point, and volume.

## Sound and looping

- Videos start **muted** so nothing blares out. Click the speaker icon in the controls to turn the sound on. Unswiper remembers your choice until you quit.
- Clips shorter than 10 seconds play in a loop.

## Live Photos

An iPhone Live Photo has a short video next to the still picture. To play that motion when you open the photo in the viewer:

1. Click the **View** menu at the top of the screen.
2. Click **Play Live Photo Motion** (a check mark shows it's on).

It is **off** when you first start Unswiper; then the viewer shows the still picture.

## If a video won't play

You'll see **This Video Can't Be Played Here**. Click **Open in Default App** to open it in your usual video player instead.

Decisions are still only carried out when you click **Review & commit…** on the visit.
